# BigMathh Tracker

A free deck tracker for **Origins TCG**, made by [BigMathGaming](https://www.youtube.com/@BigMathGaming).
Fan-made, not affiliated with Koin Games.

- **Your deck:** how many copies of each card are left and the odds of drawing it next.
- **Your opponent:** their whole deck as `? ? ?` slots that fill in as they play cards, their legendary,
  and their commander and rank (read on the VS screen).
- **Stats** in your browser: win rate per deck, card performance, matchups, ranks, locations, match history.
- **OBS:** the same panels as a browser source for streaming.

## Install (2 minutes)

1. Download **BigMathhTracker-Setup.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/mathieupelo/bigmathh-tracker-releases/releases/latest).
2. Double-click it. Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* because the app is free and not signed:
   click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click **Install**. No admin rights needed. You get a desktop and Start menu shortcut.
4. In Origins TCG, set the display to **borderless** or **windowed** (real fullscreen hides overlays). Play.

Updates install themselves: when a new version is out, the tracker shows **Update now**.

## Safe to use?

It only looks at the picture of the game window, like a screen recorder, and reads your deck list from the
files the game keeps on your PC. It **never** reads or changes the game's memory, never clicks or types in the
game, and sends nothing anywhere. Your stats stay on your PC (`%APPDATA%\BigMathhTracker`).

Uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > BigMathh Tracker > Uninstall.
